## Building digital solutions for growing businesses

**Darmi Solutions** is a Finland-based software development company focused on helping businesses build, modernize, and scale their digital presence. We design and develop responsive websites, custom web applications, backend services, and AI-enabled products tailored to real business needs.

Our approach combines practical engineering, thoughtful design, and scalable architecture. Whether a business needs its first professional website or a tailored application that simplifies daily operations, we aim to deliver reliable software that is easy to use and maintain.

## What we build

| Area | Focus |

## Our products and initiatives

### DarmiHire — Modern hiring, powered by AI

**DarmiHire** is an AI-powered recruitment and applicant tracking platform currently in development. It is designed to help organizations manage job postings, candidate applications, and recruitment workflows through a streamlined experience.

The product is evolving through active development. Features, release plans, and availability may change as the platform progresses.

## How we work

- **Understand the problem:** Clarify business goals, user needs, and delivery requirements.
- **Design with purpose:** Build intuitive experiences that work across devices.
- **Engineer for maintainability:** Favor clear code, secure defaults, and dependable integrations.
- **Deliver iteratively:** Test, refine, and improve based on feedback.
- **Support growth:** Plan for future enhancements without unnecessary complexity.
